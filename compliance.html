Legal Compliance & Policies

Zero-Tolerance Policy

In accordance with City of Dallas Transportation-for-Hire Ordinance SEC. 47A-2.1.6, SM LIMOUSINE LLC maintains a strict Zero-Tolerance Policy regarding the use of drugs or alcohol by chauffeurs while on duty or operating company vehicles.

Chauffeurs are strictly prohibited from being under the influence of any intoxicating substance while providing transportation services. Any report of a driver suspected of being under the influence will result in immediate suspension pending investigation and termination upon confirmation.

ADA Accessibility Statement

SM LIMOUSINE LLC is fully committed to serving all passengers, including those with disabilities, in accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). We provide equal service to individuals with disabilities at the same rates and standards as all other clients. Chauffeurs are trained to assist passengers with mobility aids and welcome service animals.

Report a Complaint (3-1-1)

To report a complaint regarding transportation-for-hire service, please contact SM LIMOUSINE at +1 (817) 723-4592. You may also report complaints directly to the City of Dallas by dialing 3-1-1 within city limits or via the Dallas 311 mobile app.
